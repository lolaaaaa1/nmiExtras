Sign in to record scores & edit event details

Only people the organizer has added as GitHub collaborators can save anything here. Everyone else can still open this page, but a save attempt is rejected by GitHub - so this is just a preview for them.
Record a score
Wipes every 2026 score - the leaderboard and Home go back to "watch out for 2026" until someone records a new one. Games (12) and departments (10) stay - just the scores clear.
Event details
Shown in the strip at the top of every page, and in this year's headline. Editing here only affects the live year.
Itinerary
One stop per line, as time | activity - e.g. 7:00 AM | Departure from office. Shown on Home, live year only.
Games
One game per line. Matched by exact name against the current list, so reordering or removing a line is safe - but renaming a game is treated as removing the old one and adding a new one, so its past scores won't carry over. Live year only.
Teams
One team per line, as team name | departments - e.g. Team 1 | CR & Accounts & Actuarial & BI. Shown on the Leaderboard's Pairings section, live year only.
Game pairings
One matchup per line, as round | game | team A | team B - e.g. 1 | GAME 1 | HSP | CR. Rounds are grouped and shown in order on the Leaderboard's "Who plays who" section, live year only.
Gallery photos
Review uploads in your Cloudinary Media Library first - anything approved, copy its image URL from there and paste it here, one per line, to make it go live on the Gallery page. (A plain filename works too, if you've added a file directly to galleryPhotos/2026/ in the repo instead.) Removing a line here just un-publishes it - the original stays wherever it is.
Marquee photos
A separate list from Gallery, for the scrolling strip on Home. Paste Cloudinary image URLs here, one per line. Leave it empty to just show the default trip photos - each link you add swaps in for the oldest one still showing, up to 10 total.
